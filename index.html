I am an environmental macroeconomist studying economic growth, technological change, and environmental policy. My research focuses on directed technical change, the non-carbon burdens of clean technologies, and the circular economy. My current interests also include how advances in AI can be modeled within endogenous growth theory and how they affect supply chains. I combine theoretical modeling with empirical policy analysis.
