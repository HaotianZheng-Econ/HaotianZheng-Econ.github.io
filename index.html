I am an environmental macroeconomist studying how economic growth, technological change, and public policy interact under multiple environmental constraints. I received my PhD in Economics from Nankai University in 2026.

My research combines dynamic general equilibrium modelling, mathematical analysis, and numerical simulation with firm- and city-level policy evaluation. I am particularly interested in directed technical change, the non-carbon burdens of clean technologies, circular economy, carbon pricing, and innovation policy.
